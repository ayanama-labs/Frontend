Image Formats

JPEG

I would choose JPEG for photographs and other images with many colors where a smaller file size is more important than perfect image quality.

PNG

I would choose PNG for images that need transparency or sharp details, such as logos, icons, and screenshots.

GIF

I would choose GIF for simple animations with a limited number of colors, especially short looping animations.

WebP

I would choose WebP for websites when I want good image quality with a smaller file size than many traditional image formats.

SVG

I would choose SVG for logos, icons, diagrams, and other graphics that need to remain sharp at any size.
